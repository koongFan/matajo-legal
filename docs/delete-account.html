For app reviewers (English)

Matajo lets users delete their account in two ways.

  • In app: 마이페이지 (My Page) → scroll to the bottom → 회원 탈퇴 (Delete account). Processed immediately.
  • By email: dogehj191223@gmail.com with the registered phone number. Processed within 10 days.

Deleted immediately: name, phone number, email, login identifier, saved addresses, profile photo (including the stored file), bank account details, business registration data, push tokens and device info, and linked social accounts.

Retained: repair requests, quotes and completion records for 5 years, and dispute records for 3 years, as required by the Korean Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce. Reviews remain visible with the author shown as "탈퇴한 고객" (withdrawn customer). All retained records have the name and contact details removed, so they cannot be traced to an individual.

To prevent abusive re-registration we keep a one-way hash of the phone number for 30 days, and of the business registration number until the restriction is lifted. The original values are never stored.

Partial deletion without deleting the account is also supported. Saved addresses can be deleted individually in 마이페이지 → 내 주소, a technician's profile photo can be replaced at any time, and any other data can be deleted on request by contacting us at the address below (processed within 10 days).
